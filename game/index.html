FOUNDRY LINE

A round-based tactical FPS in a dusk-lit industrial yard. Five attackers, five defenders, one spark charge — and gunfire where stopping to aim beats spraying.

Objective. Raiders plant the spark charge at Site A or B; Sentinels stop the plant or defuse it. Win by elimination, detonation, defusal — or the clock.
Economy. Earn money for kills and round wins, spend it in the buy menu (B) on rifles, armour, grenades and defuse kits.
Gunplay. Headshots deal bonus damage. Spread and recoil grow while moving and firing — crouch, stop, and aim down sights to land shots.
Bots. Nine bots run lanes, hold angles, investigate gunfire, plant, defuse and retrieve a dropped charge.
